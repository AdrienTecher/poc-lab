The move to watch is the two string crossings — ⑤→⑥ and ⑧→⑨. Both step up a string and back down the neck (the up-left arrows). Everything else is a straight step along one string.

You don't have to walk it in order. The numbers are one clean route for learning the sound and building speed. Once it's under your fingers, improvise freely — grab the nearest dot, on-route or hollow.

Where the neck runs out. Steps ⑫ and ⑬ sit at frets 13 and 15 — fine on a tenor, tight on a concert, past the end of a soprano. If you run out of frets, stop at ⑪ (G, fret 10) and treat that as the top.
